What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on a lobby or building directory from the first month? No listing except Office Evolution’s publishes this.
  2. Can the company name go on the office door itself, not just a monument sign? This is never published.
  3. Is the office available now, and is it month-to-month with no minimum? Ask for the month-to-month price in writing.
  4. Is the office on the ground floor, a short walk from the front entrance and parking?
  5. Is the front desk staffed during business hours, will mail addressed to the suite be accepted, and can someone confirm the address if Google asks for verification?

How this was put together

Business confirmed. The 24/7 phone number on arrorestoration.com, (816) 328-8934, matches the Brandon Shively contact for Arro Restoration in monday.com. The site describes water damage, mold, fire and storm restoration across the Kansas City metro and names both Overland Park and Lee’s Summit among the cities it serves. It lists no street address; its embedded map points to Best Option Restoration SE Kansas City, and the monday contact uses that company’s email domain. Neither town was treated as already covered.

Search areas. The two towns were chosen in the request. Search demand and competitors for each are on their own pages, and the 5-mile circles are used to find offices and count nearby competitors. Overland Park is centered on the Overland Park Convention Center, 6000 College Blvd, in the middle of the city’s office corridor. Lee’s Summit is centered on City Hall, 220 SE Green St, downtown. Both centers and every office were placed with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder, and distances are straight-line miles.

Offices. Small private offices within about 5 to 6 miles of each center, found on listing sites (LiquidSpace, Office Hub, Cityfeet, Showcase, LoopNet, Crexi, CommercialCafe, CommercialSearch, CoworkingCafe, OfficeSpace.com) and operator pages, checked November 23, 2026. Ratings are green only when a listing or the operator says so; nothing was called or visited. Prices are the lowest published monthly rate for a 1 to 2 person office and may change.

Search demand. Pulled from Google Keyword Planner on November 23, 2026, for November 2025 to October 2026, Google searches only. Keyword ideas came from three seed terms (water damage, restoration, water damage restoration) with two location sets: Overland Park, Leawood, Lenexa, Olathe and Prairie Village, Kansas; and Lee’s Summit, Blue Springs, Raymore, Grain Valley and Raytown, Missouri. Each returned 1,241 ideas. Kept: terms containing “water damage”, or “restoration” together with a damage or service word. Dropped: Restoration Hardware, furniture, antique, leather, marble, wood and other-city terms, the single word “restoration”, and terms under 10 searches in every month. Because the account is not spending, Google gives each keyword a range for each month; the estimate uses Google’s own middle value for each range, and terms Google reports as the same search were counted once. The full keyword list is in a separate spreadsheet.

Competitors. BBB’s Water Damage Restoration category, searched by distance from Overland Park, Lee’s Summit, Kansas City, Olathe, Independence and Blue Springs on November 23, 2026, reading pages until results were more than 22 miles from both centers. A listing was counted if its main BBB category is water, fire or general restoration or water mitigation, or its name says water, flood, dry or restoration (roofing and gutter companies left out), and it has a street address and is not marked out of business. Addresses were placed with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (one with no match uses BBB’s own location), duplicates were merged, and a company with several addresses counts once per area. Franchise or multi-location comes from the company name or more than one address. Two Lenexa companies sit within 25 feet of the Overland Park 5-mile line and are counted inside it. BBB lists only companies with a BBB profile, so the counts are a floor; both areas were counted the same way.

Recommendation. Lee’s Summit ranks first on monthly searches per restoration company in its five cities and has fewer competitors within 5 miles of its center. Overland Park has more searches overall.
